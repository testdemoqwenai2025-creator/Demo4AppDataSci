DBiT-seq math — microfluidic barcoding via two perpendicular passes
mathematics
DBiT-seq (Liu 2020) deposits barcodes via two perpendicular microfluidic passes. Pass 1 (rows): channels A, B, C, ... deposit row-specific barcodes. Pass 2 (columns): channels 1, 2, 3, ... deposit column-specific barcodes. Each 50μm pixel gets a unique (row_barcode, col_barcode) pair — the spatial identifier. Captures mRNA (via poly-T) + protein (via antibody-DNA conjugates) per pixel.

pixel_id = (row_barcoder, col_barcodec)  ·  N_pixels = n_rows × n_cols

Two-pass microfluidic barcoding. Standard: 20×20 = 400 pixels per tissue at 50μm resolution. HD: 50×50 = 2500 pixels at 20μm.

Pass 1 (rows)

channel r → row_barcode_r

Microfluidic chip with 20 parallel channels, each depositing a unique DNA barcode. Creates 20 row-barcoded strips.

Pass 2 (columns)

channel c → col_barcode_c

Rotate tissue 90°, repeat with 20 new channels. Creates 20×20 = 400 unique (row, col) pixel barcodes.

Modern papers — DBiT-seq, spatial-CUT&Tag, Spatial ATAC-RNA, spatial WNN
The four reference methods for spatial multi-omics: (1) DBiT-seq (Liu 2020) — microfluidic RNA + protein. (2) spatial-CUT&Tag (Tian 2023) — histone marks on tissue. (3) Spatial ATAC-RNA-seq (Zhang 2023) — open chromatin + RNA. (4) Seurat v5 spatial WNN (2024) — multi-modal integration.

DBiT-seq (Liu et al. 2020, Nature Biotechnology 38): Deterministic Barcoding in Tissue. Microfluidic chip deposits barcodes in two perpendicular passes (rows + columns), creating unique spatial identifiers per 50μm pixel. Captures mRNA (via poly-T) + protein (via antibody-DNA conjugates) per pixel. First method to truly co-profile RNA + protein with spatial context. Resolution: 50μm (10-50 cells per pixel). Extended: DBiT-ATAC (chromatin), DBiT-prox (proximity ligation for protein-protein interactions).

spatial-CUT&Tag (Tian et al. 2023, Nature Methods 19): Antibody-guided Tn5 transposition on tissue sections. Antibody binds specific histone modification (H3K4me3, H3K27me3, H3K27ac, etc.), protein A-Tn5 fusion cuts nearby DNA, sequencing reads spatial position + peak. Reveals chromatin regulatory state at each spatial position — which genes are 'primed' vs 'silenced'. Resolution: sub-cellular (~5μm). Limited by antibody availability (~10 marks per tissue).

Spatial ATAC-RNA-seq (Zhang et al. 2023, Nature Biotechnology 41): Co-profiles open chromatin (ATAC via Tn5 transposase) + RNA on same tissue section. Tn5 cuts open chromatin regions, poly-T captures mRNA. Sequencing reads (spatial_barcode, ATAC_peak or gene). Reveals which genes are 'open' (accessible, ready for transcription) vs 'expressed' (actually transcribed) at each position. Resolution: ~10μm. The chromatin regulatory layer invisible to RNA-only methods.

Seurat v5 spatial WNN (Hao et al. 2024): Extends Weighted Nearest Neighbours (WNN, ADR-042) to spatial context. For each pixel: find k-NN in each modality (RNA + chromatin + protein), compute per-pixel modality weights, build combined spatial graph. Output: spatial domain assignment + modality contribution per pixel. The production integration method for spatial multi-omics — handles any number of modalities without manual alignment.

11class MultiModalGraphConvolution(nn.Module):
12    """Multi-modal spatial graph convolution with cross-attention.
13    
14    Extends STAGATE (ADR-041) to multi-modal: RNA + chromatin + protein.
15    Each modality has its own encoder; cross-attention integrates.
16    """
17    def __init__(self, rna_dim: int, chrom_dim: int, protein_dim: int,
18                 hidden_dim: int = 64, n_heads: int = 4):
19        super().__init__()
20        self.n_heads = n_heads
21        
22        # Modality-specific encoders
23        self.rna_encoder = nn.Linear(rna_dim, hidden_dim)
24        self.chrom_encoder = nn.Linear(chrom_dim, hidden_dim)
25        self.protein_encoder = nn.Linear(protein_dim, hidden_dim)
26        
27        # Cross-attention: Q from modality A, K/V from modality B
28        self.rna_to_chrom_attn = nn.MultiheadAttention(hidden_dim, n_heads, batch_first=True)
29        self.chrom_to_protein_attn = nn.MultiheadAttention(hidden_dim, n_heads, batch_first=True)
30        self.protein_to_rna_attn = nn.MultiheadAttention(hidden_dim, n_heads, batch_first=True)
31        
32        # Spatial graph attention (same as STAGATE ADR-041)
33        self.spatial_attn = nn.Linear(hidden_dim * 2, n_heads)
34        self.spatial_proj = nn.Linear(hidden_dim, hidden_dim)
35        
36        self.norm = nn.LayerNorm(hidden_dim)
37    
38    def forward(self, rna: torch.Tensor, chrom: torch.Tensor, protein: torch.Tensor,
39                edge_index: torch.Tensor) -> torch.Tensor:
40        """Multi-modal spatial graph attention forward pass.
41        
42        Args:
43            rna: (N, rna_dim) RNA expression per pixel
44            chrom: (N, chrom_dim) chromatin marks per pixel
45            protein: (N, protein_dim) protein levels per pixel
46            edge_index: (2, E) spatial neighbour edges
47        
48        Returns: (N, hidden_dim) joint latent embedding
49        """
50        # Encode each modality
51        h_rna = self.rna_encoder(rna)  # (N, hidden)
52        h_chrom = self.chrom_encoder(chrom)
53        h_protein = self.protein_encoder(protein)
54        
55        # Cross-attention (RNA informed by chromatin)
56        # Need (B, N, H) format for MultiheadAttention
57        h_rna_informed, _ = self.rna_to_chrom_attn(
58            h_rna.unsqueeze(0), h_chrom.unsqueeze(0), h_chrom.unsqueeze(0)
59        )
60        h_rna_informed = h_rna_informed.squeeze(0)  # (N, hidden)
61        
62        # Cross-attention (chromatin informed by protein)
63        h_chrom_informed, _ = self.chrom_to_protein_attn(
64            h_chrom.unsqueeze(0), h_protein.unsqueeze(0), h_protein.unsqueeze(0)
65        )
66        h_chrom_informed = h_chrom_informed.squeeze(0)
67        
68        # Cross-attention (protein informed by RNA)
69        h_protein_informed, _ = self.protein_to_rna_attn(
70            h_protein.unsqueeze(0), h_rna.unsqueeze(0), h_rna.unsqueeze(0)
71        )
72        h_protein_informed = h_protein_informed.squeeze(0)
73        
74        # Joint latent: average of informed embeddings
75        joint = (h_rna_informed + h_chrom_informed + h_protein_informed) / 3  # (N, hidden)
76        
77        # Spatial graph attention (same as STAGATE)
78        src, dst = edge_index
79        msg = joint[src]  # (E, hidden)
80        agg = torch.zeros_like(joint)
81        agg.index_add_(0, dst, msg)
82        # Self + neighbours
83        combined = torch.cat([joint, agg], dim=-1)  # (N, 2*hidden)
84        attn_weights = F.softmax(self.spatial_attn(combined), dim=-1)  # (N, n_heads)
85        # Aggregate with attention
86        h_out = self.spatial_proj(joint) + 0.1 * agg
87        h_out = self.norm(h_out)
88        
89        return h_out

97    
98    Architecture:
99        Encoders: 3 modality-specific (RNA + chromatin + protein)
100        Cross-attention: integrates modalities
101        Joint latent: shared embedding
102        Decoders: 3 modality-specific (reconstruct each input)
103    
104    Loss: sum of per-modality reconstruction + KL
105    """
106    def __init__(self, rna_dim: int, chrom_dim: int, protein_dim: int,
107                 latent_dim: int = 30, hidden_dim: int = 64,
108                 n_heads: int = 4, n_layers: int = 2):
109        super().__init__()
110        self.n_layers = n_layers
111        
112        # Encoder: multi-modal graph attention layers
113        self.encoder_layers = nn.ModuleList([
114            MultiModalGraphConvolution(
115                rna_dim if i == 0 else hidden_dim,

117                protein_dim if i == 0 else hidden_dim,
118                hidden_dim, n_heads

120            for i in range(n_layers)
121        ])
122        
123        # Project to latent
124        self.fc_mu = nn.Linear(hidden_dim, latent_dim)
125        self.fc_logvar = nn.Linear(hidden_dim, latent_dim)
126        
127        # Decoders: per-modality
128        self.rna_decoder = nn.Sequential(
129            nn.Linear(latent_dim, hidden_dim),
130            nn.ReLU(),
131            nn.Linear(hidden_dim, rna_dim),
132        )
133        self.chrom_decoder = nn.Sequential(
134            nn.Linear(latent_dim, hidden_dim),
135            nn.ReLU(),
136            nn.Linear(hidden_dim, chrom_dim),
137        )
138        self.protein_decoder = nn.Sequential(
139            nn.Linear(latent_dim, hidden_dim),
140            nn.ReLU(),
141            nn.Linear(hidden_dim, protein_dim),
142        )
143    
144    def encode(self, rna: torch.Tensor, chrom: torch.Tensor, protein: torch.Tensor,
145               edge_index: torch.Tensor) -> Tuple[torch.Tensor, torch.Tensor]:
146        """Encode multi-modal inputs → joint latent (μ, logvar)."""
147        h_rna, h_chrom, h_protein = rna, chrom, protein
148        for layer in self.encoder_layers:
149            h = layer(h_rna, h_chrom, h_protein, edge_index)
150            # Update all modalities to joint (after first layer, all become hidden_dim)
151            h_rna = h
152            h_chrom = h
153            h_protein = h
154        mu = self.fc_mu(h)
155        logvar = self.fc_logvar(h)
156        return mu, logvar
157    
158    def reparameterize(self, mu: torch.Tensor, logvar: torch.Tensor) -> torch.Tensor:
159        """Reparameterisation trick."""
160        std = torch.exp(0.5 * logvar)
161        eps = torch.randn_like(std)
162        return mu + eps * std
163    
164    def decode(self, z: torch.Tensor) -> Dict[str, torch.Tensor]:
165        """Decode joint latent → per-modality reconstructions."""
166        return {
167            'rna': self.rna_decoder(z),
168            'chrom': self.chrom_decoder(z),
169            'protein': self.protein_decoder(z),
170        }
171    
172    def forward(self, rna: torch.Tensor, chrom: torch.Tensor, protein: torch.Tensor,
173                edge_index: torch.Tensor) -> Dict[str, torch.Tensor]:
174        """Forward pass for training."""
175        mu, logvar = self.encode(rna, chrom, protein, edge_index)
176        z = self.reparameterize(mu, logvar)
177        recon = self.decode(z)
178        return {
179            'latent': z,
180            'mu': mu,
181            'logvar': logvar,
182            'recon_rna': recon['rna'],
183            'recon_chrom': recon['chrom'],
184            'recon_protein': recon['protein'],
185        }
186    
187    def loss(self, rna: torch.Tensor, chrom: torch.Tensor, protein: torch.Tensor,
188             edge_index: torch.Tensor) -> torch.Tensor:
189        """Multi-modal VAE loss: sum of per-modality recon + KL."""
190        out = self.forward(rna, chrom, protein, edge_index)
191        
192        # Per-modality reconstruction (MSE)
193        recon_rna = F.mse_loss(out['recon_rna'], rna)
194        recon_chrom = F.mse_loss(out['recon_chrom'], chrom)
195        recon_protein = F.mse_loss(out['recon_protein'], protein)
196        
197        # KL divergence
198        kl = -0.5 * torch.sum(1 + out['logvar'] - out['mu'].pow(2) - out['logvar'].exp(), dim=-1).mean()
199        
200        return recon_rna + recon_chrom + recon_protein + 1e-4 * kl

204# ============================================================

206class SpatialWNN(nn.Module):
207    """Spatial WNN — extends Weighted Nearest Neighbours to spatial multi-omics.
208    
209    For each pixel: find k-NN in each modality, compute per-pixel modality weights,
210    build combined spatial graph for domain identification.
211    
212    Production: Seurat v5 spatial extension (2024).
213    """
214    def __init__(self, rna_dim: int, chrom_dim: int, protein_dim: int,
215                 embed_dim: int = 30, k: int = 10):
216        super().__init__()
217        self.k = k
218        
219        # Modality projectors
220        self.rna_proj = nn.Linear(rna_dim, embed_dim)
221        self.chrom_proj = nn.Linear(chrom_dim, embed_dim)
222        self.protein_proj = nn.Linear(protein_dim, embed_dim)
223        
224        # Per-pixel modality weights
225        self.weight_scorer = nn.Sequential(
226            nn.Linear(embed_dim * 3, embed_dim),
227            nn.ReLU(),
228            nn.Linear(embed_dim, 3),  # (w_rna, w_chrom, w_protein)
229        )
230    
231    def forward(self, rna: torch.Tensor, chrom: torch.Tensor, protein: torch.Tensor,
232                spatial_edge_index: torch.Tensor) -> Dict[str, torch.Tensor]:
233        """Compute spatial WNN graph.
234        
235        Args:
236            rna: (N, rna_dim) RNA expression per pixel
237            chrom: (N, chrom_dim) chromatin marks per pixel
238            protein: (N, protein_dim) protein levels per pixel
239            spatial_edge_index: (2, E) spatial neighbour edges
240        
241        Returns: dict with 'wnn_graph' (N, N), 'modality_weights' (N, 3)
242        """
243        # Project to common space
244        rna_proj = F.normalize(self.rna_proj(rna), dim=-1)  # (N, embed)
245        chrom_proj = F.normalize(self.chrom_proj(chrom), dim=-1)
246        protein_proj = F.normalize(self.protein_proj(protein), dim=-1)
247        
248        # Per-pixel modality weights
249        combined = torch.cat([rna_proj, chrom_proj, protein_proj], dim=-1)  # (N, 3*embed)
250        weights = F.softmax(self.weight_scorer(combined), dim=-1)  # (N, 3)
251        
252        # Compute per-modality similarities (N, N)
253        sim_rna = rna_proj @ rna_proj.T
254        sim_chrom = chrom_proj @ chrom_proj.T
255        sim_protein = protein_proj @ protein_proj.T
256        
257        # WNN: weighted combination
258        # w_pixel = (w_rna, w_chrom, w_protein) per pixel
259        w = weights.unsqueeze(1)  # (N, 1, 3)
260        sim_stack = torch.stack([sim_rna, sim_chrom, sim_protein], dim=-1)  # (N, N, 3)
261        wnn = (w * sim_stack).sum(dim=-1)  # (N, N)
262        
263        # Mask to spatial neighbours (only consider spatial k-NN)
264        # Build adjacency from spatial_edge_index
265        N = rna.shape[0]
266        adj = torch.zeros(N, N, device=rna.device)
267        src, dst = spatial_edge_index
268        adj[src, dst] = 1.0
269        wnn_spatial = wnn * adj  # only spatial neighbours contribute
270        
271        return {'wnn_graph': wnn_spatial, 'modality_weights': weights}

DBiT-seq math — microfluidic barcoding via two perpendicular passes
mathematics
DBiT-seq (Liu 2020) deposits barcodes via two perpendicular microfluidic passes. Pass 1 (rows): channels A, B, C, ... deposit row-specific barcodes. Pass 2 (columns): channels 1, 2, 3, ... deposit column-specific barcodes. Each 50μm pixel gets a unique (row_barcode, col_barcode) pair — the spatial identifier. Captures mRNA (via poly-T) + protein (via antibody-DNA conjugates) per pixel.

pixel_id = (row_barcoder, col_barcodec)  ·  N_pixels = n_rows × n_cols

Two-pass microfluidic barcoding. Standard: 20×20 = 400 pixels per tissue at 50μm resolution. HD: 50×50 = 2500 pixels at 20μm.

Modern papers — DBiT-seq, spatial-CUT&Tag, Spatial ATAC-RNA, spatial WNN
The four reference methods for spatial multi-omics: (1) DBiT-seq (Liu 2020) — microfluidic RNA + protein. (2) spatial-CUT&Tag (Tian 2023) — histone marks on tissue. (3) Spatial ATAC-RNA-seq (Zhang 2023) — open chromatin + RNA. (4) Seurat v5 spatial WNN (2024) — multi-modal integration.

DBiT-seq (Liu et al. 2020, Nature Biotechnology 38): Deterministic Barcoding in Tissue. Microfluidic chip deposits barcodes in two perpendicular passes (rows + columns), creating unique spatial identifiers per 50μm pixel. Captures mRNA (via poly-T) + protein (via antibody-DNA conjugates) per pixel. First method to truly co-profile RNA + protein with spatial context. Resolution: 50μm (10-50 cells per pixel). Extended: DBiT-ATAC (chromatin), DBiT-prox (proximity ligation for protein-protein interactions).

spatial-CUT&Tag (Tian et al. 2023, Nature Methods 19): Antibody-guided Tn5 transposition on tissue sections. Antibody binds specific histone modification (H3K4me3, H3K27me3, H3K27ac, etc.), protein A-Tn5 fusion cuts nearby DNA, sequencing reads spatial position + peak. Reveals chromatin regulatory state at each spatial position — which genes are 'primed' vs 'silenced'. Resolution: sub-cellular (~5μm). Limited by antibody availability (~10 marks per tissue).

Spatial ATAC-RNA-seq (Zhang et al. 2023, Nature Biotechnology 41): Co-profiles open chromatin (ATAC via Tn5 transposase) + RNA on same tissue section. Tn5 cuts open chromatin regions, poly-T captures mRNA. Sequencing reads (spatial_barcode, ATAC_peak or gene). Reveals which genes are 'open' (accessible, ready for transcription) vs 'expressed' (actually transcribed) at each position. Resolution: ~10μm. The chromatin regulatory layer invisible to RNA-only methods.

Seurat v5 spatial WNN (Hao et al. 2024): Extends Weighted Nearest Neighbours (WNN, ADR-042) to spatial context. For each pixel: find k-NN in each modality (RNA + chromatin + protein), compute per-pixel modality weights, build combined spatial graph. Output: spatial domain assignment + modality contribution per pixel. The production integration method for spatial multi-omics — handles any number of modalities without manual alignment.

11class MultiModalGraphConvolution(nn.Module):
12    """Multi-modal spatial graph convolution with cross-attention.
13    
14    Extends STAGATE (ADR-041) to multi-modal: RNA + chromatin + protein.
15    Each modality has its own encoder; cross-attention integrates.
16    """
17    def __init__(self, rna_dim: int, chrom_dim: int, protein_dim: int,
18                 hidden_dim: int = 64, n_heads: int = 4):
19        super().__init__()
20        self.n_heads = n_heads
21        
22        # Modality-specific encoders
23        self.rna_encoder = nn.Linear(rna_dim, hidden_dim)
24        self.chrom_encoder = nn.Linear(chrom_dim, hidden_dim)
25        self.protein_encoder = nn.Linear(protein_dim, hidden_dim)
26        
27        # Cross-attention: Q from modality A, K/V from modality B
28        self.rna_to_chrom_attn = nn.MultiheadAttention(hidden_dim, n_heads, batch_first=True)
29        self.chrom_to_protein_attn = nn.MultiheadAttention(hidden_dim, n_heads, batch_first=True)
30        self.protein_to_rna_attn = nn.MultiheadAttention(hidden_dim, n_heads, batch_first=True)
31        
32        # Spatial graph attention (same as STAGATE ADR-041)
33        self.spatial_attn = nn.Linear(hidden_dim * 2, n_heads)
34        self.spatial_proj = nn.Linear(hidden_dim, hidden_dim)
35        
36        self.norm = nn.LayerNorm(hidden_dim)
37    
38    def forward(self, rna: torch.Tensor, chrom: torch.Tensor, protein: torch.Tensor,
39                edge_index: torch.Tensor) -> torch.Tensor:
40        """Multi-modal spatial graph attention forward pass.
41        
42        Args:
43            rna: (N, rna_dim) RNA expression per pixel
44            chrom: (N, chrom_dim) chromatin marks per pixel
45            protein: (N, protein_dim) protein levels per pixel
46            edge_index: (2, E) spatial neighbour edges
47        
48        Returns: (N, hidden_dim) joint latent embedding
49        """
50        # Encode each modality
51        h_rna = self.rna_encoder(rna)  # (N, hidden)
52        h_chrom = self.chrom_encoder(chrom)
53        h_protein = self.protein_encoder(protein)
54        
55        # Cross-attention (RNA informed by chromatin)
56        # Need (B, N, H) format for MultiheadAttention
57        h_rna_informed, _ = self.rna_to_chrom_attn(
58            h_rna.unsqueeze(0), h_chrom.unsqueeze(0), h_chrom.unsqueeze(0)
59        )
60        h_rna_informed = h_rna_informed.squeeze(0)  # (N, hidden)
61        
62        # Cross-attention (chromatin informed by protein)
63        h_chrom_informed, _ = self.chrom_to_protein_attn(
64            h_chrom.unsqueeze(0), h_protein.unsqueeze(0), h_protein.unsqueeze(0)
65        )
66        h_chrom_informed = h_chrom_informed.squeeze(0)
67        
68        # Cross-attention (protein informed by RNA)
69        h_protein_informed, _ = self.protein_to_rna_attn(
70            h_protein.unsqueeze(0), h_rna.unsqueeze(0), h_rna.unsqueeze(0)
71        )
72        h_protein_informed = h_protein_informed.squeeze(0)
73        
74        # Joint latent: average of informed embeddings
75        joint = (h_rna_informed + h_chrom_informed + h_protein_informed) / 3  # (N, hidden)
76        
77        # Spatial graph attention (same as STAGATE)
78        src, dst = edge_index
79        msg = joint[src]  # (E, hidden)
80        agg = torch.zeros_like(joint)
81        agg.index_add_(0, dst, msg)
82        # Self + neighbours
83        combined = torch.cat([joint, agg], dim=-1)  # (N, 2*hidden)
84        attn_weights = F.softmax(self.spatial_attn(combined), dim=-1)  # (N, n_heads)
85        # Aggregate with attention
86        h_out = self.spatial_proj(joint) + 0.1 * agg
87        h_out = self.norm(h_out)
88        
89        return h_out

95class MultiModalSTAGATE(nn.Module):
96    """Multi-modal STAGATE autoencoder for spatial multi-omics.
97    
98    Architecture:
99        Encoders: 3 modality-specific (RNA + chromatin + protein)
100        Cross-attention: integrates modalities
101        Joint latent: shared embedding
102        Decoders: 3 modality-specific (reconstruct each input)
103    
104    Loss: sum of per-modality reconstruction + KL
105    """
106    def __init__(self, rna_dim: int, chrom_dim: int, protein_dim: int,
107                 latent_dim: int = 30, hidden_dim: int = 64,
108                 n_heads: int = 4, n_layers: int = 2):
109        super().__init__()
110        self.n_layers = n_layers
111        
112        # Encoder: multi-modal graph attention layers
113        self.encoder_layers = nn.ModuleList([
114            MultiModalGraphConvolution(
115                rna_dim if i == 0 else hidden_dim,
116                chrom_dim if i == 0 else hidden_dim,
117                protein_dim if i == 0 else hidden_dim,
118                hidden_dim, n_heads
119            )
120            for i in range(n_layers)
121        ])
122        
123        # Project to latent
124        self.fc_mu = nn.Linear(hidden_dim, latent_dim)
125        self.fc_logvar = nn.Linear(hidden_dim, latent_dim)
126        
127        # Decoders: per-modality
128        self.rna_decoder = nn.Sequential(
129            nn.Linear(latent_dim, hidden_dim),
130            nn.ReLU(),
131            nn.Linear(hidden_dim, rna_dim),
132        )
133        self.chrom_decoder = nn.Sequential(
134            nn.Linear(latent_dim, hidden_dim),
135            nn.ReLU(),
136            nn.Linear(hidden_dim, chrom_dim),
137        )
138        self.protein_decoder = nn.Sequential(
139            nn.Linear(latent_dim, hidden_dim),
140            nn.ReLU(),
141            nn.Linear(hidden_dim, protein_dim),
142        )
143    
144    def encode(self, rna: torch.Tensor, chrom: torch.Tensor, protein: torch.Tensor,
145               edge_index: torch.Tensor) -> Tuple[torch.Tensor, torch.Tensor]:
146        """Encode multi-modal inputs → joint latent (μ, logvar)."""
147        h_rna, h_chrom, h_protein = rna, chrom, protein
148        for layer in self.encoder_layers:
149            h = layer(h_rna, h_chrom, h_protein, edge_index)
150            # Update all modalities to joint (after first layer, all become hidden_dim)
151            h_rna = h
152            h_chrom = h
153            h_protein = h
154        mu = self.fc_mu(h)
155        logvar = self.fc_logvar(h)
156        return mu, logvar
157    
158    def reparameterize(self, mu: torch.Tensor, logvar: torch.Tensor) -> torch.Tensor:
159        """Reparameterisation trick."""
160        std = torch.exp(0.5 * logvar)
161        eps = torch.randn_like(std)
162        return mu + eps * std
163    
164    def decode(self, z: torch.Tensor) -> Dict[str, torch.Tensor]:
165        """Decode joint latent → per-modality reconstructions."""
166        return {
167            'rna': self.rna_decoder(z),
168            'chrom': self.chrom_decoder(z),
169            'protein': self.protein_decoder(z),
170        }
171    
172    def forward(self, rna: torch.Tensor, chrom: torch.Tensor, protein: torch.Tensor,
173                edge_index: torch.Tensor) -> Dict[str, torch.Tensor]:
174        """Forward pass for training."""
175        mu, logvar = self.encode(rna, chrom, protein, edge_index)
176        z = self.reparameterize(mu, logvar)
177        recon = self.decode(z)
178        return {
179            'latent': z,
180            'mu': mu,
181            'logvar': logvar,
182            'recon_rna': recon['rna'],
183            'recon_chrom': recon['chrom'],
184            'recon_protein': recon['protein'],
185        }
186    
187    def loss(self, rna: torch.Tensor, chrom: torch.Tensor, protein: torch.Tensor,
188             edge_index: torch.Tensor) -> torch.Tensor:
189        """Multi-modal VAE loss: sum of per-modality recon + KL."""
190        out = self.forward(rna, chrom, protein, edge_index)
191        
192        # Per-modality reconstruction (MSE)
193        recon_rna = F.mse_loss(out['recon_rna'], rna)
194        recon_chrom = F.mse_loss(out['recon_chrom'], chrom)
195        recon_protein = F.mse_loss(out['recon_protein'], protein)
196        
197        # KL divergence
198        kl = -0.5 * torch.sum(1 + out['logvar'] - out['mu'].pow(2) - out['logvar'].exp(), dim=-1).mean()
199        
200        return recon_rna + recon_chrom + recon_protein + 1e-4 * kl

206class SpatialWNN(nn.Module):
207    """Spatial WNN — extends Weighted Nearest Neighbours to spatial multi-omics.
208    
209    For each pixel: find k-NN in each modality, compute per-pixel modality weights,
210    build combined spatial graph for domain identification.
211    
212    Production: Seurat v5 spatial extension (2024).
213    """
214    def __init__(self, rna_dim: int, chrom_dim: int, protein_dim: int,
215                 embed_dim: int = 30, k: int = 10):
216        super().__init__()
217        self.k = k
218        
219        # Modality projectors
220        self.rna_proj = nn.Linear(rna_dim, embed_dim)
221        self.chrom_proj = nn.Linear(chrom_dim, embed_dim)
222        self.protein_proj = nn.Linear(protein_dim, embed_dim)
223        
224        # Per-pixel modality weights
225        self.weight_scorer = nn.Sequential(
226            nn.Linear(embed_dim * 3, embed_dim),
227            nn.ReLU(),
228            nn.Linear(embed_dim, 3),  # (w_rna, w_chrom, w_protein)
229        )
230    
231    def forward(self, rna: torch.Tensor, chrom: torch.Tensor, protein: torch.Tensor,
232                spatial_edge_index: torch.Tensor) -> Dict[str, torch.Tensor]:
233        """Compute spatial WNN graph.
234        
235        Args:
236            rna: (N, rna_dim) RNA expression per pixel
237            chrom: (N, chrom_dim) chromatin marks per pixel
238            protein: (N, protein_dim) protein levels per pixel
239            spatial_edge_index: (2, E) spatial neighbour edges
240        
241        Returns: dict with 'wnn_graph' (N, N), 'modality_weights' (N, 3)
242        """
243        # Project to common space
244        rna_proj = F.normalize(self.rna_proj(rna), dim=-1)  # (N, embed)
245        chrom_proj = F.normalize(self.chrom_proj(chrom), dim=-1)
246        protein_proj = F.normalize(self.protein_proj(protein), dim=-1)
247        
248        # Per-pixel modality weights
249        combined = torch.cat([rna_proj, chrom_proj, protein_proj], dim=-1)  # (N, 3*embed)
250        weights = F.softmax(self.weight_scorer(combined), dim=-1)  # (N, 3)
251        
252        # Compute per-modality similarities (N, N)
253        sim_rna = rna_proj @ rna_proj.T
254        sim_chrom = chrom_proj @ chrom_proj.T
255        sim_protein = protein_proj @ protein_proj.T
256        
257        # WNN: weighted combination
258        # w_pixel = (w_rna, w_chrom, w_protein) per pixel
259        w = weights.unsqueeze(1)  # (N, 1, 3)
260        sim_stack = torch.stack([sim_rna, sim_chrom, sim_protein], dim=-1)  # (N, N, 3)
261        wnn = (w * sim_stack).sum(dim=-1)  # (N, N)
262        
263        # Mask to spatial neighbours (only consider spatial k-NN)
264        # Build adjacency from spatial_edge_index
265        N = rna.shape[0]
266        adj = torch.zeros(N, N, device=rna.device)
267        src, dst = spatial_edge_index
268        adj[src, dst] = 1.0
269        wnn_spatial = wnn * adj  # only spatial neighbours contribute
270        
271        return {'wnn_graph': wnn_spatial, 'modality_weights': weights}

Insight
RNA-only spatial transcriptomics (ADR-041) shows what's expressed. Spatial multi-omics adds the regulatory layer: WHY genes are expressed (chromatin marks) and WHAT they do (protein levels). The three layers — regulatory (chromatin) → expression (RNA) → functional (protein) — form the complete causal chain. This is the multi-modal RAG (ADR-033) applied to spatial biology — three modalities co-registered in one embedding space.

Spatial multi-omics adds the regulatory layer biology was missing. RNA-only methods (Visium, MERFISH — ADR-041) show what genes are expressed at each spatial position. But they miss WHY: is the gene 'primed' (H3K4me3 at promoter, ready for transcription) or 'silenced' (H3K27me3 covering gene body, Polycomb-repressed)? Spatial-CUT&Tag answers this. And the protein layer (DBiT-seq antibody-DNA conjugates) shows WHAT the expressed genes actually DO — many RNA changes don't translate to protein (post-transcriptional regulation). The three layers — regulatory → expression → functional — form the complete causal chain. Spatial multi-omics measures all three simultaneously on the same tissue. This is the regulatory layer biology has been missing since the central dogma (Crick 1958): DNA → RNA → protein, now measured in spatial context.
