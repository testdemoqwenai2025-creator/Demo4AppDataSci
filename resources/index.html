1873

First derivation of the singular value decomposition A = UΣV^T. Independently rediscovered by Jordan 1874. Foundational for PCA, image compression, genomics.

1874

Independent derivation of SVD (same year as Beltrami). The modern formulation stems from Jordan's bilinear forms paper.

→ cited on /living-svdopen

1936

Theorem: the best rank-k approximation of A (in Frobenius norm) is U_k Σ_k V_k^T. Basis for PCA and truncated SVD.

→ cited on /living-svdopen

2017

Introduced scaled dot-product attention, replacing RNNs/LSTMs. The most-cited AI paper of the decade. Foundation of GPT-4, AlphaFold2, ESM-2.

1805

1965

An algorithm for the machine calculation of complex Fourier series. Reduced DFT from O(N²) to O(N log N) — enabled digital signal processing.

→ cited on /living-fftopen

Poisson — Recherches sur la probabilité des jugements (1837)

1837

Derived the Poisson distribution as the limit of Binomial(N, p) with N→∞, p→0, λ=Np fixed. The universal law of rare events.

1948

Introduced Shannon entropy H = -Σ p log p as the unique measure of uncertainty. Foundation of information theory. Bell System Technical Journal.

Boltzmann — Beziehung zwischen dem zweiten Hauptsatze (1877)

1877

S = k·log(W) — Boltzmann's entropy. The 2nd law of thermodynamics: entropy always increases. Same formula as Shannon (1948), different domain.

→ cited on /living-entropyopen

1918

Heterozygosity H = 1 - Σ p² as genetic diversity measure. Related to Shannon entropy via H ≈ 1 - e^(-S). Foundation of population genetics.

1973

C = S·N(d1) − K·e^(-rT)·N(d2). 1973 Nobel Prize in Economics (with Merton). Foundation of all modern option pricing.

1805

Edmund Bowring derived the haversine formula hav(Δσ) = sin²(Δσ/2) to avoid catastrophic cancellation in the spherical law of cosines for small angles.

Papers — every mathematical derivation cited
20 papers
The 20 papers cited across the 10 living-equation Math tabs. From Beltrami 1873 (SVD) to Brin & Page 1998 (PageRank), each citation is the original derivation of the equation featured on a living-equation page. Click through to read the primary source.

Beltrami — Sulle funzioni bilineari (1873)

1873

First derivation of the singular value decomposition A = UΣV^T. Independently rediscovered by Jordan 1874. Foundational for PCA, image compression, genomics.

→ cited on /living-svdopen

Jordan — Mémoire sur les formes bilinéaires (1874)

1874

Independent derivation of SVD (same year as Beltrami). The modern formulation stems from Jordan's bilinear forms paper.

→ cited on /living-svdopen

Eckart & Young — Approximation of one matrix by another (1936)

1936

Theorem: the best rank-k approximation of A (in Frobenius norm) is U_k Σ_k V_k^T. Basis for PCA and truncated SVD.

→ cited on /living-svdopen

Vaswani et al. — Attention Is All You Need (2017)

2017

Introduced scaled dot-product attention, replacing RNNs/LSTMs. The most-cited AI paper of the decade. Foundation of GPT-4, AlphaFold2, ESM-2.

→ cited on /living-attentionopen

Jumper et al. — AlphaFold2 (Nature 2021)

2021

CASP14 GDT_TS 92.4 — AlphaFold2's structure head IS conditional diffusion. Evoformer: 48 attention blocks × 4 heads.

→ cited on /living-attentionopen

Gauss — Cooley-Tukey FFT predecessor (1805, posthumous)

1805

Carl Friedrich Gauss derived the FFT (Fast Fourier Transform) decades before Cooley-Tukey, in an unpublished note on asteroid orbit computation.

→ cited on /living-fftopen

Cooley & Tukey — FFT algorithm (1965)

1965

An algorithm for the machine calculation of complex Fourier series. Reduced DFT from O(N²) to O(N log N) — enabled digital signal processing.

→ cited on /living-fftopen

Poisson — Recherches sur la probabilité des jugements (1837)

1837

Derived the Poisson distribution as the limit of Binomial(N, p) with N→∞, p→0, λ=Np fixed. The universal law of rare events.

→ cited on /living-poissonopen

Shannon — Mathematical Theory of Communication (1948)

1948

Introduced Shannon entropy H = -Σ p log p as the unique measure of uncertainty. Foundation of information theory. Bell System Technical Journal.

→ cited on /living-entropyopen

Boltzmann — Beziehung zwischen dem zweiten Hauptsatze (1877)

1877

S = k·log(W) — Boltzmann's entropy. The 2nd law of thermodynamics: entropy always increases. Same formula as Shannon (1948), different domain.

→ cited on /living-entropyopen

Haldane — Mendel class ratios (1918)

1918

Heterozygosity H = 1 - Σ p² as genetic diversity measure. Related to Shannon entropy via H ≈ 1 - e^(-S). Foundation of population genetics.

→ cited on /living-entropyopen

Black, Scholes — Pricing of Options (J. Political Economy 1973)

1973

C = S·N(d1) − K·e^(-rT)·N(d2). 1973 Nobel Prize in Economics (with Merton). Foundation of all modern option pricing.

→ cited on /living-black-scholesopen

Merton — Theory of Rational Option Pricing (Bell J. 1973)

1973

Independent derivation of Black-Scholes + extension to American options + dividend-paying stocks. Shared 1973 Nobel with Black & Scholes.

→ cited on /living-black-scholesopen

Bowring — Haversine (1805)

1805

Edmund Bowring derived the haversine formula hav(Δσ) = sin²(Δσ/2) to avoid catastrophic cancellation in the spherical law of cosines for small angles.

→ cited on /living-haversineopen

Kalman — New Approach to Linear Filtering (ASME 1960)

1960

A New Approach to Linear Filtering and Prediction Problems. Foundation of optimal estimation. Apollo navigation used Kalman for lunar module (1969).

→ cited on /living-kalmanopen

Metropolis & Ulam — Monte Carlo Method (JASA 1949)

1949

The Monte Carlo Method. Invented at Los Alamos (Manhattan Project) for neutron-transport calculations. E[f(X)] ≈ (1/N)·Σ f(X_i).

→ cited on /living-monte-carloopen

Boyle — Monte Carlo for Options (J. Financial Econ. 1977)

1977

First application of MC to option pricing. 10⁶ GBM paths → ±$0.01 SPX call. Now industry standard for path-dependent options.

→ cited on /living-monte-carloopen

Bachelier — Théorie de la spéculation (1900)

1900

First application of Brownian motion to finance. Pre-Black-Scholes (73 years). Used additive noise — allowed negative prices (Samuelson fixed in 1965).

→ cited on /living-gbmopen

Samuelson — Rational Theory of Warrant Pricing (1965)

1965

Introduced multiplicative noise (σS·dW) — fixed Bachelier's negative-price problem. Foundation of GBM and Black-Scholes (1973).

→ cited on /living-gbmopen

Brin & Page — PageRank (1998)

1998

PR(p) = (1-d) + d·Σ(PR(q)/L(q)). The original Google algorithm. Now measures systemic risk (BIS), port centrality (UN COMTRADE), gene essentiality (STRING).

→ cited on /living-entropyopen
